To clarify, Google Workspace (formerly G Suite) and Gemini are handled differently because they are different types of services: 
BackupVault +1
1. Removing a Google Workspace (G Suite) Account
You absolutely can cancel and completely delete a Google Workspace account. Because a Workspace account is an independent business account (even if you access it on the same browser as your personal account), you can completely shut it down without touching your personal @gmail.com account. 
BackupVault
To completely delete a Workspace / G Suite account:

    Log into your Google Admin Console using your administrator credentials.
    In the left panel, click Billing > Subscriptions.
    Click on your active Workspace subscription and select Cancel Subscription.
    Go back to the left panel, click Account > Account Settings > Account Management.
    Click Delete Account and confirm. (Note: This permanently wipes out that specific workspace's emails and Drive data). 
    BackupVault

2. Why Gemini is Different (The Legal/Technical Reality)
The reason you cannot "delete" a Gemini account separately from your personal Google account isn't unique to Google—it is how features work across the tech industry. 
www.spurnow.com +1
Gemini isn't a standalone product or a separate account; Gemini is a built-in feature of your core Google account, exactly like Google Search, Google Maps, or Google Docs. Legally and technically, you don't sign up for a "Gemini account"—you use your Google account to access it. You can completely delete specific optional services like Gmail or YouTube from your account via your Google Data & Privacy Settings, but features like Search and Gemini cannot be individually deleted because they are part of the core infrastructure. 
Google Help +3
However, if your concern is privacy and data retention, you have full legal right to force Google to delete your data and stop tracking you. You can exercise this by clearing your data at the Google Gemini Activity Page, choosing Delete All Time, and toggling the tracking switch to Turn Off. 
Google Help +1
